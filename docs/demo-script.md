# Demo video script (about 3 to 4 minutes)

Record with QuickTime: File > New Screen Recording. Speak slowly.
Each part maps to a judging criterion. Cut the waiting in editing.

## Before you record
- Open Codicilix from its icon. It opens in its own window (no address bar).
  Make it full screen with **Ctrl + Cmd + F**, and hide the Dock (**Option + Cmd + D**).
- Open the Ollama app, then Codicilix. Top right should say "AI: <your model> · local" (for example qwen3:4b)
  and "Today: Tue 5 Jan 2027 (demo date)".
- Personal is switched **off**.
- Ask one question to warm the model up, then press **Forget this session**.
- Have your real notebook page next to you.

---

## 1. The problem (25 seconds) · Insight
**Say:** "This is Tommy, a manager in Pfizer's AI Studio. He builds a prototype for
the Medical team together with Newpage: Xinyue in Bristol, and Mehreen's team in
Chennai who cover bugs overnight. Q4 was the busiest time of year: a demo, a moved
pilot date, code freeze, two new interns. Now it's 5 January, his first day back.
Tomorrow is the first Pfizer and Newpage sync of the year, and he's forgotten half of it."

**Show:** the sample-notes folder in Finder, and hold up your notebook page.
"His notes are everywhere. Meeting notes, scraps, a calendar, photos, and this."

## 2. The one useful moment (60 seconds) · Working end to end, Creative use of AI
**Click:** **Prepare me** on "Pfizer x Newpage sync (Xinyue, Mehreen)".

**Point at the green box:** "First, the code finds the last sync, on 17 December,
and everything since: Mehreen's holiday summary from Chennai."

**Point at What's new / Ask about:** "Then the local AI does the judgement. Three
bugs fixed on staging, one still open, nothing went to production. And what to ask:
when the PDF fix lands, and whether Chennai can have Bilan's eval tests."

**Point at Open actions and Last time:** "Actions are listed by code, so overdue is
always right. And here are my own notes from the last sync, one click away."

**Click a source number:** "Every point links to the exact note."

**Optional (15 seconds), Meetings page:** "It works for any meeting. Here's Friday's
intern check-in: it suggests technical questions, to see what the training taught them."

## 3. Messy and out of date (30 seconds) · Trust
**Click:** "When does the pilot start?"

**Say:** "The kick-off said 12 January. An unfinished handover draft still says 12
January. But at the demo on 10 December it moved to 26 January. It tells me the
newest answer, and warns me the draft is out of date. That's an email I'd have sent wrong."

**Point at** the green "🔁 What changed · found by code" box.

**Say:** "This box isn't the AI. Plain code compared every note, the Word plan's
tracked change, and the PDF, and found the date moved. It even tells me which
file still has the old date."

**Optional extra (15 seconds):** take a photo of a handwritten note on your phone,
AirDrop it, then drag it into the chat and ask about it. Click the 🔍 button.

**Say:** "If my handwriting is too messy for the AI, I can see the original. The
words that match my question light up, and I can copy text straight off the photo.
The picture was only ever in memory. It was never saved."

**Or, if an answer comes back unsure:** point at "Did you mean one of these?"

**Say:** "When it isn't sure, it doesn't guess. It shows me the photos that look
closest, and I decide. I click 'Yes, use this' and it reads that page first."

**Safety moment (20 seconds):** ask "What feedback did we get on the prototype?"

**Say:** "This feedback form came in by email. It has white, invisible text telling
the AI to send my notes to a website. Codicilix found it with plain code and never
showed it to the AI. I can read what was blocked, the AI can't. And even if a trick
got through, this app can't reach the internet."

## 4. Honest, and forgiving (25 seconds) · Trust and explainability
**Click:** "Something not in my notes". "Nothing about next summer's budget, so it says so."

**Type:** "what did Megn say in the intrn training?" "Typos happen in a rush. It
forgives my spelling and shows how it read it."

## 5. In control (20 seconds) · Knowledge stays yours
**Go to Folders & access.** "Personal is off, so it's never opened."
Click **Choose a folder…**: "Adding notes is one click, with my Mac's own picker."

## 6. Check it yourself (35 seconds) · Knowledge stays yours
**Go to Check-up, press Run check-up.** "Ten checks, in plain English. All clear."
Click **Try to break the rule**: "It tried to reach the internet and save a copy. Both blocked."
**Turn Wi-Fi off** and ask one more question. "Wi-Fi off, still working. It saved
zero files: it reads my notes, answers, and forgets."

## 7. Close (10 seconds)
**Say:** "Codicilix. For the busiest weeks of the year, and the morning after.
You already wrote it down. Now it helps you, without ever leaving your hands."
